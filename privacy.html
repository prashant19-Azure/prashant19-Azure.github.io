PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This is a starter privacy notice for the Prashant AI Automation website. Review and adapt it to your actual business practices and applicable law before collecting real visitor information.

Information you may submit

If the enquiry form is connected and enabled, it may send your name, email address, optional phone number, company name, selected topic, message, and submission time to the business systems configured for this website.

Why information is used

Information should be used only to respond to your enquiry, understand the requested service, and carry out appropriate follow-up. Do not submit passwords, one-time passcodes, payment-card details, or sensitive identity documents.

Where information goes

When configured, the website may transmit enquiry information to an n8n webhook and then to business tools such as a spreadsheet or email service. The operator must confirm the actual destinations, access controls, retention period, and deletion process before enabling collection.

Chat

The chat widget is a demo until connected to a live backend. If enabled, messages may be sent to the configured automation service for generating a response. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Contact and requests

To ask about information you have submitted, contact the business using the official contact channel shared by Prashant AI Automation. Add your verified business email or other contact details here before publishing.
